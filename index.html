3Droid

An experimental Android emulator for Android.

Welcome

This is the web entry point for the 3Droid PWA. You can install it to your home screen and launch it like a normal app.

How to install

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Edge on Android.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Launch 3Droid from your home screen.
